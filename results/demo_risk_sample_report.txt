Demo risk sample report
========================

Method
------
Every verified mapping row was checked against the source image SHA-256. The existing CycloneTrackPredictor LSTM and CycloneRiskAssessor were used without modifying model weights, thresholds, mapping data, IBTrACS data, or risk calculations. Selected images were then uploaded through the real /api/analyze application pipeline.
