AI-Based Cyclone Early Warning System

A North Indian Ocean research platform for historical-track forecasting, explainable risk assessment, and experimental INSAT-3D satellite-product analysis.

Launch Dashboard

System Capabilities

Comprehensive pipeline for cyclone monitoring

Satellite Analysis

Previews INSAT-3D products and, when trained, classifies product type only. The supplied images have no verified storm or intensity labels.

Track Prediction

Employs LSTM sequential modeling to forecast cyclone track (Lat/Lon) and intensity up to 48 hours ahead.

Risk Assessment

Rule-based expert system evaluates wind, pressure, and trajectory to generate actionable risk scores.

Interactive Map

Real-time geospatial visualization with Leaflet.js rendering observed and forecasted cyclone paths.

Developed for Smart India Hackathon (SIH)
