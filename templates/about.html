About the Project

Methodology

The AI-Based Cyclone Early Warning System combines advanced deep learning techniques to provide comprehensive cyclone analysis:

  • Image Classification (CNN): Convolutional Neural Networks analyze satellite imagery (e.g., INSAT-3D IR) to classify current cyclone intensity.
  • Track Prediction (LSTM): Long Short-Term Memory networks process sequential historical data (latitude, longitude, wind speed, pressure) to forecast the future trajectory.
  • Risk Assessment Logic: Rule-based systems evaluate predicted parameters to generate actionable early warnings.

Limitations & Disclaimer

This system is a prototype developed for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH). It is NOT an operational meteorological tool. Predictions made by this system are for demonstration purposes only. For actual weather warnings and cyclone information, always refer to the official reports from the India Meteorological Department (IMD) or local authorities.
